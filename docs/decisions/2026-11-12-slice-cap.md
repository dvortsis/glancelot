# Up to 12 slices at once

Written for: the lead and every slice session.

**Decision (user, November 12, 2026):** "up to 12 slices as long as work is available." This replaces the four-session cap in AGENTS.md (user decision, November 4, 2026) until AGENTS.md itself is updated in a reviewed PR. It also lifts the November 5 `new_slices_paused` key.

**What doesn't change:**
- At most three local builds, and none while a full gate holds the gate lock.
- One full gate at a time on this PC.
- Each slice still needs a fresh review and the full gate before it merges, and only the lead merges.

**How it's applied:**
- Only slices whose dependencies have merged are started. The cycle on November 12 starts nine (`docs/handoff/PHASE3-CYCLE1-2026-11-12.md`). With A1 and B1 still open, that makes eleven, and B3-FOLLOWUPS is held for a file conflict until PR 7 merges.
- New implementers run as cloud sessions, under the AGENTS.md local lead topology, so they don't take local build slots.
- Reviewers and the tester stay local.

**Cost:** reviews and the serial gate become the bottleneck, so PRs wait longer between going ready and merging. The lead batches two to four approved PRs per gate to keep up.
